THREE-YEAR WORK SCHEDULE - FULL DATA FILE
Period: 12 October 2026 to 11 October 2029 (3 plan years, 12 quarters, 36 plan months)
Format note for tools: each section below has a fixed header (YEAR n / QUARTER / MONTH) followed by one paragraph. A 'plan month' runs from the 12th of one month to the 11th of the next. Dates are written as DD Month YYYY.
====================================================================================================

GLOBAL RULES (APPLY TO ALL 3 YEARS)

Working hours and days: Regular working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday, which is 9 hours on site including 1 hour of lunch and two 15-minute breaks, so about 8 hours are planned as work time (480 planned minutes). Saturdays are reserved for optional light work or learning for no more than two hours (suggested 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM, skipped freely if rest is needed). Sundays are rest days with no planned work. Overtime is not a routine; unfinished work is carried forward instead.

Daily routine: 09:00-09:30 planning and choosing the three most important priorities of the day. 09:30-11:30 focused work session one. 11:30-11:45 scheduled break. 11:45-01:00 PM implementation. 01:00-02:00 PM lunch. 02:00-03:30 PM collaboration, meetings, reviews and communication. 03:30-03:45 PM scheduled break. 03:45-05:15 PM focused work session two or testing. 05:15-06:00 PM end-of-day review, in which I record actual time, blockers, completed tasks and tomorrow's carried-forward items and tick the daily checklist.

Weekday theme: Monday is for planning weekly deliverables and setting targets. Tuesday and Wednesday are for focused development or project work. Thursday is for testing results, validation and fixing issues. Friday is for reviewing progress, updating documentation and preparing the next week. Every week the planned deliverable has its due date on Friday.

Time limits and tracking: I set a realistic time limit for every task before starting it, track planned versus actual minutes, document every blocker with its cause and date, and carry unfinished work forward when necessary. A task that is carried forward is marked 'carried_forward = Yes' and must be finished within its expiry window.

Deadlines and expiry: Each workday task has a due date (the Friday of that week) and an expiry date (three calendar days after the due date, i.e. the following Monday). If a task is not completed or re-planned by its expiry date it is flagged as EXPIRED and must be reviewed at the next daily planning session, then either rescheduled with a new realistic date or dropped with a recorded reason. Saturday and Sunday records expire on the same day.

Status values: Pending, In Progress, Completed, Carried Forward, Blocked, Expired, Skipped, N/A (used for Sundays).

Monthly review: At the end of each plan month, on the last working day, I review completed tasks, deadlines met and missed, quality, time estimates against actual time, and unresolved issues, then set realistic priorities for the next month.

Quarterly review: Every three months I conduct a broader review of progress, skills, project outcomes, workload and goals, and adjust the plan where needed. A quarterly review replaces the normal Friday review on that day and is allowed to use the whole afternoon.

Tracking tools: I maintain a daily checklist, a task tracker, and a monthly progress record so that my work remains organized and measurable. The data files that go with this document are daily_schedule_3yr.csv, weekly_plan_3yr.csv, monthly_review_3yr.csv and quarterly_review_3yr.csv.

Measures of success: percentage of planned tasks completed on time, planned versus actual minutes, number of blockers and how long they stayed open, overtime days (target close to zero), rest days actually taken, number of features shipped, defects found in testing, documentation pages updated, and number of new skills practised.

Assumptions: Public holidays, leave and personal events are not included in this schedule; when one falls on a workday it is marked 'Skipped' and its tasks are carried forward. The year 2028 includes 29 February.

YEAR 1 (12 October 2026 to 11 October 2027)

I will follow a structured one-year work schedule from 12 October 2026 to 11 October 2027, with regular working hours from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekdays.
